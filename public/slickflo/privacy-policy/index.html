Information we collect

We collect only what you send us. When you complete the contact form we receive your name, email address, the service you selected and your message. We do not collect payment information through this website.

How we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and scope the work you have asked about

We do not sell your information, and we do not share it with third parties for marketing purposes.

Enquiries are kept for as long as needed to respond and, where an engagement follows, for the duration of our business relationship plus any period required by law. You can ask us to delete your details at any time.

You can request a copy of the information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Write to slickflo.co@gmail.com and we will respond within 30 days.
