Frequently Asked Questions

What is an SOP, and why have one written up?

A standard operating procedure records how a task is actually done, so the process survives staff changes instead of living in one person's head.

Do you write SOPs for non-technical teams?

Yes. SOPs cover operational processes across a business, not only technical ones.

How do you capture how our team really works?

By working with the people who do the task, following the approach on this page.
