Delivery

The room changes the course.

Courses and workshops are designed around how they will be delivered. A session in a room and the same session over video are not one course with a different joining link, so we design them apart from the start.

Instructor-Led Training (ILT)

Delivered in person, in a room. The instructor can read the group and change pace on the spot, so the material is built to let them: exercises that run on a whiteboard, and handouts people can write on.

Virtual Instructor-Led Training (VILT)

The same live session, delivered remotely to people in different places. Nobody can read a room over video, so attention has to be designed in: shorter segments, a deliberate interaction every few minutes, and material that works on one screen beside the call.

Frequently Asked Questions

What do your training modules include?

Structured modules and courses built for retention and consistency, with the supporting material a learner works through.

Can you set these up in our LMS?

Yes. LMS configuration is part of this service.

Do you follow instructional design standards?

Yes. We build SCORM-compliant material and work to established frameworks; there is more detail on the Learning & Development page.

Should our training be delivered in person or virtually?

It depends on who is attending and where they are. Instructor-Led Training (ILT) puts everyone in one room, which suits hands-on work and groups who can travel to it. Virtual Instructor-Led Training (VILT) runs the same live session over video for people in different places, which suits teams spread across sites or time zones. We design the course around whichever you need, because the two are not the same material with a video link added.
