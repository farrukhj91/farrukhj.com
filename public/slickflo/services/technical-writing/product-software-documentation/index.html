Release Notes

Release notes are written from the build, not from the ticket.

A ticket says what was meant to change. Release notes have to say what actually did. We work the new features the way a customer will, confirm what shipped against what was specified, and write the note from that. It is why release notes sit with QA rather than beside them, and why the note matches the build people download.
