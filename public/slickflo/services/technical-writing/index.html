The basics

So, what is technical writing?

Technical writing turns complex technical, product, and process information into clear information people can understand and act upon.

For software companies, that can mean explaining a feature, helping a developer integrate an API, creating a user guide, documenting product requirements, communicating a release, or organizing knowledge that already exists but is difficult to use.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is technical writing and why does my business need it?

Technical writing turns product, technical and process knowledge into information people can understand and act on. For a software company that is the difference between a user who finds an answer and a user who opens a support ticket. Good technical documentation reduces repeated questions, lowers the load on support and engineering, and makes the product easier to adopt.

What types of technical documentation does SlickFlo create?

User guides and user documentation, knowledge base documentation, API documentation and API manuals for developer audiences, product and software documentation such as PRDs, specifications and release notes, standard operating procedures, and proposal documentation. The What do we offer section on this page lists each service and links to its own page.

Can SlickFlo document an existing software product?

Yes. Most of our work starts with a product that already exists and documentation that has not kept pace with it. We get familiar with the product, its audience and its workflows, work from your existing material and time with your subject matter experts, and build the software documentation from there rather than asking your engineers to draft it first.

Can you update or migrate our existing technical documentation?

Yes. Documentation migration is one of our technical writing services. We move documentation between platforms while preserving structure, accessibility and continuity, and we can audit, reorganize and update what you already have rather than starting again. If the problem is that the content is scattered rather than missing, that is usually where we begin.

How does SlickFlo ensure technical documentation is accurate?

We learn the product before writing about it, then check the documentation against it. That means working from the product itself alongside your subject matter experts, and reviewing whether a reader can complete the task the page describes. We care about whether the documentation works, not only whether it reads well, and we work in the platforms your team already uses.
