Frequently Asked Questions

How is a knowledge base different from user guides?

A guide walks someone through a task from start to finish. A knowledge base is structured for search, because the reader arrives with one specific question and should not have to read the rest.

Can a knowledge base reduce our support load?

That is what the structure is for: making answers findable so the same questions stop reaching the support queue.

Do you build internal knowledge bases as well as customer-facing ones?

Yes, both.
