Documentation Migration Services

Change platforms without losing the archive

Seamless migration of documentation systems into structured, scalable, and easy-to-maintain platforms without losing clarity or context. Our documentation migration services move help centers, knowledge bases and product docs between platforms with links, structure and history intact.

Our Approach Talk to Us

Modernizing Documentation Without Losing Structure

As organizations scale, documentation often becomes fragmented across outdated systems, multiple tools, and inconsistent formats, making it difficult to manage and maintain.

SlickFlo helps organizations migrate and restructure their documentation into modern, scalable platforms that improve usability, consistency, and long-term maintainability.

From legacy documentation systems and fragmented knowledge bases to outdated content structures, our content migration services ensure smooth transitions into organized, accessible, and well-structured documentation environments.

This is documentation work, not a bulk data transfer. Every article is checked, restructured where needed and kept accurate, whether that is a knowledge base migration between help center platforms or moving documentation from Confluence into a custom WordPress setup your team can manage.

Our Approach

1

Assess

We inventory your existing documentation, its structure, links and formatting, and decide what to move, merge or retire.

2

Map

We map old content and URLs to the new platform’s structure.

3

Migrate

We move the content, converting formats and rebuilding navigation, tables, images and cross-links in the new platform.

4

Standardize

We apply consistent templates, terminology and formatting across the migrated content.

5

Optimize

We test everything in a staging environment, then check links, search and navigation after launch, and fix gaps before your team and customers rely on it.

Case Studies

01

Confluence to WordPress Documentation Migration

A client was dissatisfied with the visual presentation and limited customization options of their existing Confluence documentation platform. They wanted a more flexible, visually engaging documentation experience that could be managed directly through their WordPress website.

SlickFlo designed a custom HTML documentation template, secured client approval, and migrated the existing content from Confluence while preserving all documentation and ensuring no data was lost.

We developed a custom WordPress backend using Advanced Custom Fields (ACF) for bulk documentation uploads and built an Elementor widget that enabled the client’s team to easily create, edit, and manage documentation pages.

The entire solution was tested in a staging environment before deployment to the live website, ensuring a smooth and reliable migration.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does a documentation migration involve?

Moving your documentation from one platform to another without breaking links, losing history, or disrupting how your team works day to day. It covers auditing what you have, mapping it to the new structure, moving and reformatting the content, testing in staging, and checking everything after launch.

Will our existing links stop working?

Preserving links and history is part of the migration, not something patched up afterwards.

Can you migrate and restructure at the same time?

Yes. A migration is a natural moment to improve structure, and the approach on this page covers both together.
