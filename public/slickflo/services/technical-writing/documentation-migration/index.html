Frequently Asked Questions

What does a documentation migration involve?

Moving your documentation between platforms without breaking links, losing history, or disrupting how your team works day to day.

Will our existing links stop working?

Preserving links and history is part of the migration, not something patched up afterwards.

Can you migrate and restructure at the same time?

Yes. A migration is a natural moment to improve structure, and the approach on this page covers both together.
