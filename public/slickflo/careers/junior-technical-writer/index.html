Junior Technical Writer

Full timeRawalpindi / Islamabad (Hybrid)Posted 28 October 2026

Overview

SlickFlo is looking for a dynamic Jr. Technical Writer who can create technical documentation such as user manuals, user guides and knowledgebase articles for the SaaS based Ed-tech industry.

Responsibilities

  • Research, develop, and write user guides, tutorials, and other documentation for EdTech platforms and Learning Management Systems (LMS).
  • Collaborate with product, design, and development teams to ensure technical accuracy and consistency across all content.
  • Create and maintain engaging, concise, and user-friendly content for diverse audiences, including students, educators, and administrators.
  • Incorporate feedback from stakeholders to edit and refine content for clarity, usability, and alignment with project goals.
  • Ensure all documentation adheres to established style guides, brand voice, and accessibility standards.
  • Design visual aids such as diagrams, screenshots, and workflows to enhance user understanding.
  • Contribute to knowledge-sharing initiatives, team training sessions, and internal content strategies.
  • Attend cross-functional team meetings and stay available during working hours as required.
  • Stay informed on the latest industry trends, pedagogical methods, and emerging technologies in EdTech through independent research.

Requirements

  • Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, Electrical Engineering, English Literature or a related field.
  • 3 to 12 months of experience in technical writing: creating user guides and knowledgebase articles.
  • Familiarity or experience with learning management systems and the ed-tech industry is a plus.
  • The candidate should be comfortable using multiple project management and documentation tools.
  • Familiarity with documentation tools such as WordPress, Confluence, Markdown tools, or similar.
  • Familiarity with JIRA and other project management tools is a plus.
  • Exceptional written and verbal communication skills with the ability to explain complex technical concepts in simple terms.
  • Excellent writing, editing, and proofreading skills.
  • Strong attention to detail and ability to meet deadlines.
  • The candidate should demonstrate the ability to learn quickly, work independently, and collaboratively.

Please Note

This is a full time hybrid position, combining work from the company’s office with remote work. Fresh graduates are encouraged to apply.

How to Apply

Please send your resume to hr.slickflo@gmail.com. The subject line must read Candidate Name - Jr. Technical Writer, with your own name in place of Candidate Name. If you have prior writing experience, please attach your technical writing samples.

Applications close on 30 November 2026.
