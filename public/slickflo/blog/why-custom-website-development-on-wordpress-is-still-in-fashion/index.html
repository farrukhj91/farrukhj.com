Why Custom Website Development on WordPress is Still in Fashion

By Amna Baig, Technical Writer at SlickFlo25 June 2025

Businesses often face a dilemma: Should they invest in modern full-stack frameworks like MERN or MEAN, or stick with trusted platforms like WordPress? Especially a small business owner, finally ready to put out their brand online. You talk to a few developers and they start throwing around buzzwords; React, Angular, Node.js.

With every new JavaScript framework release, it’s easy to assume WordPress has lost its charm. Yet, statistics tell another story; over 40% of the world’s websites still run on WordPress.

As a matter of fact, WordPress is not just about “drag-and-drop” themes or cookie-cutter templates anymore. Using custom website development on WordPress, businesses can accomplish swift, scalable, and versatile websites without burning through their budget or resources. Let’s explore why this approach remains in fashion, even in 2025.

Potential Drawbacks of using Modern Stacks

Some contemporary stacks as MEAN (MongoDB, Express.js, Angular, Node.js) and MERN (MongoDB, Express.js, React, Node.js) are exceptionally potent. They give developers the ability to create feature-rich online apps with sophisticated backends, real-time updates, and advanced features. However, there's a catch:

  • Higher Costs: Hiring full-stack developers with expertise in these frameworks is significantly more expensive than working with WordPress developers.
  • Lengthier Timelines: Using MERN/MEAN to create a website from the ground up might take weeks or even months. All features, including content management and user authentication, need to be coded.
  • Resource-intensive: Projects based on these stacks frequently call for participation from QA engineers, developers, and perhaps DevOps experts. For a startup or small business, this can cause expenses to mount up rapidly.
  • Maintenance Overhead: Keeping a custom-built MERN/MEAN site secure and updated requires ongoing attention, which again adds to expenses.

Modern stacks are completely justified for large-scale applications such as marketplaces or SaaS platforms. However, this may not be the greatest plan of action if the ultimate objective is to create a quick, optimized, business-ready website.

Why choose Custom Web Development Using WordPress?

Here’s where WordPress excels. It offers a strong foundation right out of the box, in contrast to contemporary stacks that frequently reinvent the wheel. There are already built-in core functions including media handling, user management, SEO optimization, and a dashboard-based CMS. Developers can concentrate on personalization instead of starting from zero.

For example:

  • A small business wanting a website with a services section, blog, and contact form can have it live within days instead of months. Look at The Walt Disney Company’s corporate site clean, professional, and content-focused, all built on WordPress.
  • Custom functionality like an appointment booking system or membership portal, can be developed faster using plugins or tailored PHP code. For instance, Harvard Gazette, Harvard University’s official news site built on WordPress, manages a massive content system with flexibility and ease, something that would take far longer if built from scratch on a modern stack.
  • Even e-commerce is covered: brands like Bata run their online stores on WordPress (with WooCommerce), showing how quickly businesses can scale from simple sites to full online sales platforms.

This time efficiency translates directly into lower costs and faster go-to-market timelines, making WordPress a smart choice for projects with limited budgets and resources.

At SlickFlo, we take a consultative approach. We carefully evaluate customer requirements and then propose the right stack. If the project needs heavy custom logic or enterprise-level apps, we may suggest modern stacks. But if the requirements can be achieved efficiently with WordPress, we advocate for it—because it saves our clients time, resources, and unnecessary costs without compromising on quality.

Features of Websites Built Using WordPress Custom Development

Personalized WordPress websites are anything but simple. They can be as sophisticated and well-developed as any full-stack application with the help of talented developers. Here are a few noteworthy characteristics:

1. Speed

Custom-built WordPress websites can perform significantly better. By avoiding heavy themes and unnecessary plugins, which are popular with both users and search engines, developers ensure fast loading time.

2. Optimization

For optimal visibility and use, WordPress websites may be optimized for anything from mobile adaptability to SEO-friendly structures. Optimization is simple with tools like server-level caching and Yoast SEO.

3. Easy Deployment

WordPress may be set up with just a hosting server and database, in contrast to contemporary stacks that can require cloud orchestration or containerization. Deployment is further simplified by WordPress hosting services.

4. Flexibility

Custom themes and plugins allow businesses to shape their site however they want, be it for a portfolio, an online store, or a learning management system.

5. Scalability

WordPress websites may grow to accommodate high traffic volumes if they have the proper architecture. For this reason, WordPress is used by e-commerce sites, online magazines, and high-traffic news websites all over the world.

Conclusion

There is no doubt that modern stacks like MERN and MEAN play a significant role in creating intricate, extensive applications. For many firms, however, the objective is quite straightforward: a budget-friendly, speedy, secure website. Here, WordPress custom website creation continues to be the best option.

WordPress provides the ideal blend of functionality and performance by fusing flexibility with time efficiency. If you’re planning a new website and want to maximize value without unnecessary complexity, custom website development is exactly what you need.
